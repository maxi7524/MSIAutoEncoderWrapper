Introduction

Data: mass spectrometry images (MALDI)

intuition

  • every pixel of the image carries a whole mass spectrum
  • MALDI: the tissue section is scanned pixel by pixel; one spectrum per position

Data: mathematical description

formal

Image \(\quad I \subset \mathbb{N}^{2}\) (or \(\mathbb{N}^{3}\)), pixel \(p = (x, y)\)

Spectrum at \(p\) — peaks \((m, v) \in \mathbb{R}_+ \times \mathbb{R}_+\) \[\mu_p = \sum_k v_k\, \delta_{m_k}\]

Binning of the m/z axis \([100, 3000]\) with step \(0.55\) \[B_i = [\,100 + 0.55(i-1),\ 100 + 0.55\,i\,), \quad i = 1, \dots, M\] \[x_{p,i} = \sum_{m_k \in B_i} v_k, \qquad M = 5273\]

\[\text{data} \;\subset\; I \times \prod_{i=1}^{M} \big(B_i \times \mathbb{R}_+\big) \;\cong\; I \times \mathbb{R}_+^{M}, \qquad x_p \mapsto \frac{x_p}{\lVert x_p \rVert_1} \in \Delta^{M-1} \ \ (\text{TIC})\]

Segmentation: where the word atlas comes from

intuition

  • the globe is known, but we work with charts: approximate, direct representations in \(\mathbb{R}^2\)
  • an atlas is a collection of charts \((U_\alpha, \varphi_\alpha)\), \(\ \varphi_\alpha : U_\alpha \to \mathbb{R}^d\)

  • a capital needs several coordinates (position, name, country, …)

Segmentation: the analysed example

  • encoder compresses a spectrum into a latent point \(z\)
  • decoder reconstructs the spectrum from \(z\)
  • goal: construct \(z\) so that it is stable and meaningful

Segmentation: the atlas, formally

formal

\[\mathrm{Enc}: \Delta^{M-1} \to S^{L-2}(\sqrt{L}) \subset \mathbf{1}^\perp \subset \mathbb{R}^{L}, \qquad \mathrm{Dec}: \mathbb{R}^{L} \to \Delta^{M-1}\]

Bottleneck LayerNorm of \(a = \mathrm{CNN}(x) \in \mathbb{R}^L\) \[u = \frac{a - \mu_a \mathbf{1}}{\sigma_a} \;\Rightarrow\; \mathbf{1}^\top u = 0, \quad \lVert u \rVert^2 = L\] so \(u\) lies on the sphere \(S^{L-2}(\sqrt{L})\) in \(\mathbf{1}^\perp\); \(z = \gamma \odot u + \beta\) is an affine image of it.

\(L = 10 \;\Rightarrow\; u \in S^{8}\)

Heads on the latent

Masserstein: comparing spectra

TIC-normalized spectra are probability measures on the m/z axis. We compare them by the Wasserstein distance — the cost of moving intensity along m/z.

In one dimension it is exact and linear-time: \[W_1(x, \hat x) = \int \big| F_x(m) - F_{\hat x}(m) \big|\, dm\] \[= \sum_{i=1}^{M-1} \Big| \sum_{j \le i} (x_j - \hat x_j) \Big| \,(m_{i+1} - m_i)\]

  • no transport matrix, no iterative solver: \(O(M)\)
  • a shifted peak costs its shift, not a full mismatch

Contrastive learning: invariance to peak permutations

  • a view \(\tilde x\) permutes the shapes of three unannotated peak envelopes of \(x\) (TIC preserved)
  • large points: pixels; small points: their views — the tighter the cloud, the more invariant the encoder

Contrastive learning: formally

formal

For a batch \(x_1, \dots, x_B\) and views \(\tilde x_i = T(x_i)\), projections \(g_i = g(\mathrm{Enc}(x_i))\), \(g_{B+i} = g(\mathrm{Enc}(\tilde x_i))\):

\[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{NCE}} = -\frac{1}{2B}\sum_{i=1}^{2B} \log \frac{\exp\big(\mathrm{sim}(g_i, g_{\pi(i)})/\tau\big)}{\sum_{j \ne i} \exp\big(\mathrm{sim}(g_i, g_j)/\tau\big)}, \qquad \mathrm{sim}(a, b) = \frac{a^\top b}{\lVert a \rVert \lVert b \rVert},\ \ \tau = 0.07\]

  • \(\pi(i)\): the other view of the same spectrum (positive pair); every other spectrum is a negative
  • numerator pulls the two views together, denominator pushes different spectra apart
  • \(T\): cyclic permutation of peak-envelope shapes, only peaks without annotation — the representation may ignore unannotated rearrangements, but not the annotated evidence
  • the loss acts on the projection \(g(z)\), the latent \(z\) is shaped only through the gradient

Contractive learning: formally

formal

Local sensitivity of the canonical latent: \(J_u(x) = \partial u / \partial x \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times M}\)

With \(z = \gamma \odot u + \beta\): \[J_z = \operatorname{diag}(\gamma)\,\tfrac{1}{\sigma_a}\,P\,A_x, \quad P = I - \tfrac{1}{L}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top - \tfrac{1}{L}uu^\top\] \(\lVert J_z \rVert\) can be reduced by shrinking \(\gamma\) without changing the encoder \(\Rightarrow\) we penalize \(J_u\).

Geometry of the input: spectra are points of the simplex; its natural metric is Fisher–Rao, \[\langle v, w \rangle_x = \sum_i \frac{v_i w_i}{x_i}, \qquad d_{FR}(x, x') = 2 \arccos \sum_i \sqrt{x_i x'_i}\]

\[A_x = J_u(x)\,\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{x})\,\big(I - \sqrt{x}\sqrt{x}^{\top}\big), \qquad \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{contr}} = \sigma_{\max}(A_x)^2 + \alpha\,\big[\sigma_{\max}(A_x) - \tau\big]_+^2\]

\(\sigma_{\max}\) by power iteration; \(\alpha = 2\), \(\tau = 7\). Rifai et al. (2011) ICML — Frobenius, Euclidean, unnormalized latent.
